If you've got five or more days in Istanbul, or you're a return visitor who's already covered the greatest hits, the smartest use of an extra day is leaving the city entirely. This guide (the written companion to Episode 8, the season finale of our podcast *Istanbul Unpacked*) compares the two best options — and helps you pick the right one for the time you have.

**First-time visitor with only 2–3 days?** Skip this post for now and start with our [one-day Old City guide](/blog/sultanahmet-old-city-istanbul-one-day-itinerary) instead — the city itself needs you more.

## Option 1: The Princes' Islands — No Cars, Just Horses and Pine

**What it is:** an archipelago of nine small islands in the Sea of Marmara, roughly an hour from the city by ferry. The two most-visited islands, **Büyükada** and **Heybeliada**, banned motor vehicles over a century ago — the only traffic is bicycles and horse-drawn carriages (fayton).

**Why it feels like time travel:** faded pastel Ottoman-era wooden mansions line quiet streets, pine forest climbs the hills, and the pace runs at roughly a third of Istanbul's. It's the easiest way to feel like you've left Turkey without leaving the city's ferry network.

**How the day works:**
- Morning ferry from Kabataş or Kadıköy (60–90 minutes depending on route and island)
- Rent a bicycle at the harbor, or hire a fayton for a loop around the pine-forested hills
- Coffee stop at a hilltop café overlooking the Marmara Sea
- Seafood lunch at a harbor restaurant
- Late-afternoon ferry back, timed for a golden-hour finish on the water

**Best for:** travelers wanting a genuine pace change — quiet, nature, and a slower rhythm after a full-throttle city trip.

## Option 2: Şile — The Black Sea You Didn't Know Was There

**What it is:** a small town on the Black Sea coast, about 70 km from central Istanbul — a different sea, a different coastline, an entirely different mood.

**Why it surprises people:** most visitors picture Istanbul purely as a Bosphorus-and-Marmara city. Şile flips that: dramatic cliffs, a lighthouse dating to the 1850s, and a beach-town character that feels almost Aegean, transplanted to the north. The town is also known nationally for a light cotton fabric, Şile bezi, sold in shops along the main street — a genuinely distinctive souvenir you won't find in the Grand Bazaar.

**How the day works:**
- No direct ferry — travel by bus or organized tour, roughly 90 minutes each way by road
- Morning departure, arriving by mid-morning
- Walk the lighthouse point and cliffside paths
- An hour or two on the beach
- Seafood lunch facing the Black Sea
- Browse the Şile bezi shops before the return trip

**Best for:** travelers with a genuinely full free day who want a landscape that looks nothing like the rest of their Istanbul trip, and don't mind trading city hours for road time.

## Which One Should You Choose?

| | Princes' Islands | Şile |
|---|---|---|
| Travel time | ~1–1.5 hrs each way (ferry) | ~1.5 hrs each way (road) |
| Best if you have | Half day to full day | A full free day |
| Transport | Ferry (familiar network) | Bus/tour (new territory) |
| Character | Quiet, slow, car-free | Dramatic coastline, beach town |
| Choose if you want | Pace change | Scenery change |

There's no wrong choice — only a question of how much of your remaining time you want to spend in transit versus on-site.
